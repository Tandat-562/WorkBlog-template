## General Evaluation

### 1. Learning and Working Environment

First Cloud AI Journey provides a friendly, open, and supportive learning environment that encourages participants to actively explore technology. During the program, I had opportunities to exchange knowledge with other members, participate in discussions, and support one another in resolving issues that arose during practical exercises.

The program allowed me to combine theoretical learning with hands-on practice on the AWS platform. This helped me better understand how AWS services are deployed and connected within real-world systems.

However, the program could organize more networking sessions, experience-sharing activities, or team-building events to help members connect and understand one another better.

### 2. Support from Mentors and Team Admins

The mentors and team admins provided active support throughout the program. When I encountered difficulties during labs or project implementation, I received useful guidance and suggestions that helped me continue researching and solve problems independently.

I highly appreciate the way mentors encouraged participants to conduct research, experiment, and troubleshoot issues instead of immediately providing answers. This approach helped me improve my critical thinking, documentation research, and technical troubleshooting skills.

The team admins also provided effective support by sharing information, learning materials, announcements, and progress updates.

### 3. Relevance of the Program to My Field of Study

The program content was highly relevant to my academic and career development in information technology, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence.

Through the program, I had the opportunity to study and practice with several important AWS service categories, including:

- AWS Networking.
- AWS Compute.
- AWS Storage.
- AWS Security.
- AWS Database.
- Amazon Bedrock and AI services.
- Serverless and event-driven architectures.

These topics helped me strengthen my foundational knowledge while also exposing me to technologies and architecture models commonly used in real-world systems.

### 4. Learning Opportunities and Skill Development

During my participation in First Cloud AI Journey, I gained valuable knowledge and developed new skills, especially in deploying, managing, and troubleshooting systems on AWS.

Through the labs and Capstone Project, I improved the following skills:

- Using the AWS Management Console and AWS CLI.
- Designing system architectures on AWS.
- Deploying serverless services.
- Managing access permissions with IAM.
- Monitoring resources with Amazon CloudWatch.
- Troubleshooting networking, security, and permission-related issues.
- Working in teams and assigning responsibilities.
- Tracking progress and writing technical documentation.
- Searching for, reading, and applying AWS documentation.

In particular, developing the **AI AWS Architecture Reviewer** project helped me better understand how to combine a frontend, backend, AWS services, and Amazon Bedrock to build a complete application.

### 5. Culture and Team Spirit

The members of the program demonstrated a strong willingness to learn and support one another. Whenever a participant encountered difficulties, other members were usually willing to discuss the problem and share their experience.

During the group project, team members worked together to select the topic, analyze requirements, design the architecture, and divide responsibilities. Each member was responsible for their assigned tasks and collaborated to achieve the shared objectives.

This environment helped me improve my communication, idea presentation, and teamwork skills.

### 6. Training Content and Supporting Resources

The program provided a wide range of learning materials and practical labs related to AWS services. Combining guided documentation with hands-on exercises made it easier for me to understand new concepts.

In addition, the practical assignments and final project gave me the opportunity to apply what I had learned to a specific system.

However, the program could provide a centralized resource page containing documents, videos, sample source code, and common troubleshooting issues. This would make it easier for participants to review and search for previously studied content.

## Additional Questions

### What I Was Most Satisfied With During the Program

The aspect I was most satisfied with was the opportunity to work directly with multiple AWS services and apply the knowledge to a Capstone Project.

Instead of only studying theory, I was able to deploy real resources, troubleshoot issues, and verify results on AWS. This process helped me understand how different services operate and connect within a complete architecture.

I also highly appreciated the support provided by the mentors, team admins, and other program members.

### Areas That Could Be Improved

The program could improve the following areas:

- Provide a clearer weekly learning roadmap.
- Organize more regular progress reviews and assessments.
- Add lab review sessions and explanations of common errors.
- Invite experienced AWS professionals to share practical knowledge.
- Increase networking and teamwork activities among participants.
- Build a centralized resource repository for easier access and reference.
- Organize additional project presentation and evaluation sessions before the program ends.

### Would I Recommend the Program to My Friends?

I would recommend First Cloud AI Journey to friends who are interested in cloud computing, AWS, DevOps, system engineering, and artificial intelligence.

The program is suitable for learners who want to build a strong AWS foundation, gain hands-on experience with cloud services, and participate in the development of a practical project.

However, participants should be willing to learn independently, actively search for documentation, and remain patient when troubleshooting technical issues.

## Suggestions and Expectations

### Suggestions for Improving the Program Experience

I suggest that the program consider adding:

- A detailed weekly learning roadmap and checklist.
- Regular mentor review sessions to monitor participant progress.
- Hands-on sessions focused on real-world AWS troubleshooting.
- Career orientation and AWS Certification sharing sessions.
- Advanced labs for specific career paths such as Cloud Engineering, DevOps, Security, Data, and AI.
- A mid-program project demonstration so that teams can receive early feedback.
- A final presentation and Capstone Project review session.
- Networking or team-building activities to strengthen connections among participants.

### Interest in Continuing with the Program

Given the opportunity, I would like to continue participating in advanced First Cloud AI Journey activities and programs in the future.

I am particularly interested in AWS architecture, serverless systems, DevOps, cloud security, Generative AI, and Amazon Bedrock. I would also like to participate in more practical projects to continue developing my professional skills.

### Additional Feedback

First Cloud AI Journey is a valuable program that provides practical knowledge and experience for students and beginners who want to learn about AWS.

Through the program, I not only gained cloud computing knowledge but also improved my independent learning, teamwork, progress management, technical writing, and troubleshooting skills.

I would like to express my sincere appreciation to the mentors, team admins, and other members who supported me throughout the program. The knowledge and experience I gained will serve as an important foundation for my continued development in cloud computing and artificial intelligence.
